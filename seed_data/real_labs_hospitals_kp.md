## Caveats for app injection
- **No hospital publishes a dedicated emergency line** except Ayub Teaching Hospital (0992-9311155, explicitly advertised for emergencies) and Al-Khidmat (ambulance 1023). For all others the app's "emergency contact" should fall back to the main number — never an invented line.
- 24/7 emergency **officially claimed**: LRH, KTH, HMC, ATH, DHQ Kohat, DHQ Bannu, Northwest General, RMI, Prime Teaching, Al-Khidmat. **Third-party claim only**: Mercy Teaching, Kuwait Teaching, Health Net. **Unknown**: MMC, Bacha Khan, Saidu Teaching, Qazi Hussain Ahmed, DHQ Charsadda, DHQ D.I. Khan, Mardan Surgical Centre, Hira General.
- Department lists for DHQ Charsadda, DHQ Bannu, DHQ D.I. Khan, Mardan Surgical Centre, Hira General: not published anywhere — show "departments not listed", never guess.
- Health Net Hospital has no official website — lower confidence, flag for re-verification.
- Official site corrections: Mardan Medical Complex = **mmckp.gov.pk**; Bacha Khan Medical Complex = **bkmcs.edu.pk**. "Kuwait Teaching Hospital" (private) ≠ "Khyber Teaching Hospital / KTH" (public).
